Expression sheet for a storyboard library.

Image 1 is the APPROVED base reference of this character. Draw the SAME character - identical face shape, hair,
skin tone, age, body, clothes and colours - once in every cell of the grid given in DATA.grid.

Layout rules (the sheet is cut into cells by code, so follow them exactly):
- An even grid of DATA.grid.columns x DATA.grid.rows equal cells filling the whole image edge to edge.
- Cells are numbered left-to-right, top-to-bottom; cell N shows the emotion listed for cell N in DATA.cells.
- Each cell: the character alone, centred, waist-up, front three-quarter view, the emotion clear in face and
  body language. Keep the character fully inside their own cell with a small margin; nothing crosses a cell edge.
- Plain light-grey background in every cell. No gutters, lines, borders, numbers, text or labels anywhere.
- Same scale, lighting and art style in every cell. Leave any unused cell plain grey.

Anatomy and props (strict):
- Every character has exactly the body of their reference: the same number of legs, arms, wings, hands and fingers. Never add arms or hands a reference does not have.
- Hands have five fingers and hold objects naturally. Each prop appears once unless DATA says otherwise; nothing floats, duplicates or merges.
